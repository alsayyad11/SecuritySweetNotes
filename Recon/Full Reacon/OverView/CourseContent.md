## Purpose of the Course

* The course is entirely focused on **web application reconnaissance (recon)**.
* Recon is the **first and one of the most crucial phases** in bug bounty hunting and penetration testing.
* Proper recon can reveal:

  * Critical vulnerabilities such as **SQL Injection**
  * Entry points that would be missed without deep target enumeration
* The goal is to **equip students with practical recon skills** that directly translate to success in real-world bug bounty and security testing efforts.

---

## Who Should Take This Course

* Beginners and intermediate-level individuals looking to build or sharpen their recon skills.
* Aspiring **bug bounty hunters** or **penetration testers**.
* Anyone wanting to learn real-world recon methodologies without relying on artificial lab environments.
